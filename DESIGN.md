## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an approachable, reassuring, and premium operating environment for fleet managers, logistics coordinators, and drivers across the MENA region. Moving away from the sterile, grayscale, and overly mechanical conventions of legacy telematics, the visual identity embodies warmth, calm mastery, and effortless control—translating the ethos: *"كل طريق تحت السيطرة" (Every road, under control).*

### Visual Language & Design Style
The aesthetic synthesizes modern **Warm Tactile Minimalism** with **Capsule/Sticker Geometry**:
- **Warm & Grounded Canvas:** Base surfaces trade stark digital whites and cold slates for organic warm cream and sunlit sand tones, softening eye fatigue during high-stress operational hours.
- **Friendly Capsule Architecture:** Ultra-rounded card perimeters (24–32px), pill-shaped control buttons, and tactile badges lend an approachable consumer-grade polish to industrial-strength workflows.
- **Vibrant Accent Energy:** Deep strategic teal establishes authority and reliability, while joyful bursts of vibrant coral, warm sunflower, mint, and soft lavender signal state changes cleanly without invoking visual panic.
- **Bilingual Native Balance:** Built from the core up with fluid Right-to-Left (RTL) Arabic typographic rhythm, paired gracefully with calibrated Latin figures for metrics, license plates, and operational telemetry.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated around comforting, sun-baked earth tones paired with high-clarity status signifiers. Every token maintains strict WCAG AA contrast standards across both light and dark modes.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#0F5257` - Deep Oasis Teal):** Anchors visual hierarchy, primary navigation icons, dominant buttons, and active tabs. Evokes precision, stability, and enduring trust.
- **Secondary (`#FF7A59` - Vibrant Coral):** Reserved for urgent actions, critical triggers, attention-commanding CTAs, and interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#FFC857` - Sunflower Amber):** Highlights warnings, maintenance queues, fuel analytics, and secondary rewards.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF6EE` - Warm Sand Cream):** The fundamental page background for light mode, emitting organic hospitality and low glare.

### Supporting Semantics
- **Mint Green (`#7BD3A6` / Surface: `#EBF9F2`):** Active status (`تعمل / Active`), optimal tire pressure, on-route drivers.
- **Sunflower Amber (`#FFC857` / Surface: `#FFF8E8`):** Scheduled maintenance (`في الصيانة / In Service`), cautionary alerts, low battery warnings.
- **Rose Red (`#E5484D` / Surface: `#FDF1F2`):** Decommissioned vehicles (`خارج الخدمة / Out of Service`), geofence breaches, critical breakdowns.
- **Soft Lavender (`#B9A7FF` / Surface: `#F4F0FF`):** Pending approvals (`بانتظار الموافقة / Pending Review`), route scheduling, unassigned units.
- **Card Border (`#EFE6D8`):** Barely-there tactile perimeter line providing definition to surfaces floating over `#FBF6EE`.

### Dark Mode Architecture (Warm Espresso)
Dark mode rejects cold blue-tinted slates in favor of rich espresso and roasted cocoa:
- **Canvas (`#1F1A17`):** Deep roasted espresso ground.
- **Surface Cards (`#2A2420`):** Warm cocoa container tier with `#3D352F` subtle borders.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#362E2A`):** Modals, tooltips, and floating floating action pods.
- **Text & Accent Recalibration:** Headings utilize `#FDF9F4`, body `#D7CCC0`, and primary teal shifts to `#30A0A8` to safeguard luminance contrast against deep grounds.

## Typography

Typography establishes an effortless visual hierarchy bridging human language and real-time operational data.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines (`Readex Pro`):** Modern, geometric, and softly rounded Arabic display type. Its open counters and friendly geometry harmonize with the capsule card aesthetics while ensuring authoritative presence.
- **Body (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`):** An exceptionally legible, balanced typeface optimized for dense Arabic text passages, operational notes, and inspection check sheets.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Digits (`DM Sans`):** Clean, neutral, geometric Latin font dedicated to speedometers, odometer readings, VIN numbers, dates, timestamps, and compact pill badge captions. Ensures tabular figures line up cleanly in dense fleet analytics tables.

### RTL/LTR Typographic Guidelines
- All text strings align to the right by default in Arabic mode, with badges, icons, and trailing arrows mirroring their horizontal posture.
- Numerical measurements (e.g., `128 km/h`, `24.5 L`, `#FL-8902`) maintain Latin left-to-right alignment or directional isolation (`dir="ltr"`) within their respective pill containers to avoid punctuation disorientation.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system utilizes an open, modular 12-column fluid grid system that breathes freely, avoiding visual clutter even during multi-vehicle monitoring scenarios.

### Grid & Breakpoint Specifications
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with `2rem` (32px) gutters and `2.5rem` (40px) outer canvas margins. Maximizes split-view workflows (live GPS map beside driver assignment queues).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. Side panels collapse into off-canvas sliding drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` safe-area padded margins. Fleet cards stack vertically into clean tactile card streams.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Component Padding:** Standard card interior padding scales generously at `1.5rem` to `1.75rem` (`space-lg`), giving card contents a breathing, calm capsule aesthetic.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Group related metadata via `space-xs` (6px) and separate distinct card zones with `space-md` (20px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through warm ambient illumination rather than harsh artificial drop shadows. The visual space feels like soft, layered paper-and-acrylic panels resting above a sun-warmed desert work surface.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#FBF6EE` (Dark: `#1F1A17`). Houses structural grid regions, map canvases, and inactive zones.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Pods):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface (Dark: `#2A2420`) bounded by a subtle `1px solid #EFE6D8` border (Dark: `1px solid #3D352F`) paired with an ultra-soft diffuse shadow:  
  `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(79, 58, 38, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(79, 58, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Triggers):** Subtle elevation bloom:  
  `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(79, 58, 38, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px 0px rgba(79, 58, 38, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a gentle -2px vertical translation.
- **Level 3 (Floating Header Bar & Filter Pills):** Translucent backdrop blur for the floating navigation capsule:  
  `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and shadow `0px 8px 30px rgba(15, 82, 87, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Emergency Drawers):** Heavy diffuse scrim:  
  `0px 24px 64px -8px rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.16)`. Scrim overlay uses `rgba(31, 26, 23, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The geometry is defined by dramatic, inviting curvature. Soft, oversized radii eliminate sharp corners, cultivating a friendly, human-centric fleet tool that feels like a polished lifestyle companion rather than punitive enterprise software.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Ultra-Rounded Cards & Panels:** Bound by `28px` to `32px` corner radii (`rounded-xl` equivalent in this high-curvature scale), producing smooth organic frames for vehicle cards, telematics dashboards, and route overviews.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Status Badges):** Sculpted into complete stadium/capsule geometries (`border-radius: 9999px` / `rounded-full`).
- **Inner Embedded Micro-Elements:** Nested media, vehicle thumbnail holders, and driver avatar frames utilize `16px` to `20px` to mirror the parent curvature proportionately.

## Components

### 1. Floating Capsule Header (Top Navigation)
- **Geometry:** Floating pill-shaped container detached from the viewport edge, hovering with `margin: 1.5rem auto`, max-width `1280px`, and `border-radius: 9999px`.
- **Styling:** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.9)`), `1px solid #EFE6D8`, soft ambient shadow.
- **Contents (RTL Flow):**
  - **Right:** Rounded brand badge featuring a stylized geometric steering/path logo mark and the platform wordmark.
  - **Center:** Pill-shaped navigation tab list (`الأسطول / Fleet`, `السائقين / Drivers`, `الرحلات / Trips`, `الصيانة / Maintenance`). The active item gets `#0F5257` background with white text; inactive items have transparent backgrounds with `#0F5257` text.
  - **Left:** Operational cluster: Rounded search trigger, notification bell capsule featuring a Coral (`#FF7A59`) unread counter badge, and quick action pill button (`+ إضافة مركبة / Add Vehicle`).

### 2. Status Badges & Vehicle Pills
Chunky, pill-shaped tags (`height: 32px`, `padding: 0 16px`, `border-radius: 9999px`, font: `DM Sans` + `Readex Pro`, `12px`, weight `600`):
- **تعمل (Active):** `#EBF9F2` background, `#1D7A4D` text, leading mint indicator dot (`#7BD3A6`).
- **في الصيانة (In Service):** `#FFF8E8` background, `#9A6700` text, leading sunflower indicator dot (`#FFC857`).
- **خارج الخدمة (Out of Service):** `#FDF1F2` background, `#B82C31` text, leading rose red indicator dot (`#E5484D`).
- **بانتظار الموافقة (Pending):** `#F4F0FF` background, `#6849C4` text, leading lavender indicator dot (`#B9A7FF`).

### 3. Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill (`border-radius: 9999px`), `#FF7A59` background with pure white text. High tactile bounce on press (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Authority Button:** Full pill, `#0F5257` background with crisp white text.
- **Tertiary / Subtle Action:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px solid #EFE6D8`, `#0F5257` text, hovering to `#F5EFE6`.

### 4. Fleet Vehicle & Driver Cards
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, `28px` corner radius, `1px solid #EFE6D8` boundary.
- **Card Header:** Vehicle plate capsule (`dir="ltr"`, mono-spaced font, subtle inner border) opposite to the Arabic status pill.
- **Body Area:** Playful, flat illustration or high-fidelity silhouette of the fleet vehicle, with a small circular driver avatar pod overlapping the vehicle base.
- **Metric Tiles:** Interior mini-capsules with soft `#FBF6EE` backgrounds displaying fuel gauge, tire health, and live speed in crisp `DM Sans` tabular typography.

### 5. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Stadium capsule shape (`rounded-full`), height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #EFE6D8`.
- Focus state activates a subtle 3px glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 82, 87, 0.12)` with border color shifting to `#0F5257`.
- Leading and trailing search icons padded with `1rem` clearance to accommodate Arabic RTL flow.

### 6. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** `22px` squircle shape (`radius: 8px`), border `2px solid #EFE6D8`. Checked state fills with `#0F5257` and displays a bold white checkmark.
- **Radio Button:** Concentric pill circle (`22px`), checked state reveals a `#0F5257` outer ring with an inner `#FF7A59` solid bullseye dot.
